About Fetop Haraka Limited

Fetop Haraka Limited is a leading supplier of premium branded garments and corporate wear based in Nairobi, Kenya.

We supply T-Shirts, Roundnecks, Vests, Polos, Dotted Polos, Dotted Round-necks to businesses, schools, NGOs and institutions across Kenya.

We are committed to providing the highest quality products at competitive prices with fast, reliable delivery across the country. Every order is handled with care and attention to detail.
